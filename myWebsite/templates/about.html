About Calling Darjeeling

Where your journey into the heart of the Himalayas begins

Welcome to Calling Darjeeling — where your journey into the heart of the Himalayas begins.

Rooted in the beautiful hill town of Darjeeling, we are a passionate team of locals who believe travel is more than just sightseeing — it's about discovering stories, cultures, and connections. With deep knowledge of the region and a love for its heritage, we curate unforgettable tours that help you explore Darjeeling, Kalimpong, Mirik, and the hidden gems in between.

Whether you're sipping tea on a misty hillside, watching the first light touch Mt. Kanchenjunga, or wandering through local markets, our goal is to make you feel not just like a traveler — but like a part of Darjeeling's soul.
