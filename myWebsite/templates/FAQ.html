What are the popular destinations to be explored in Sikkim and Darjeeling?

Some must-visit places include Gangtok, Tsomgo Lake, Nathula Pass, Pelling, and Yumthang Valley in Sikkim, and Tiger Hill, Batasia Loop, Mirik, and Peace Pagoda in Darjeeling.

Which is the best time to avail Darjeeling and Sikkim Tour Packages?

The ideal time to visit is from March to June and from September to early December when the weather is pleasant and perfect for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

Is Darjeeling best for a Honeymoon couple?

Absolutely! With its serene landscapes, cozy weather, and romantic views of the Himalayas, Darjeeling is a top destination for honeymooners seeking tranquility and natural beauty.

What should I pack while on tour to Sikkim or Darjeeling?

Pack warm clothes, especially from October to March. Comfortable shoes, ID proof, essential medicines, and a power bank are highly recommended. If visiting during the rainy season, carry an umbrella or raincoat.

What are the Highlights of Sikkim and Darjeeling Tour Packages?

The highlights include panoramic views of the Himalayas, Buddhist monasteries, tea gardens, cultural richness, adventure opportunities, and peaceful hill stations perfect for relaxation and exploration.

Do I need permits for visiting North Sikkim or Nathula Pass?

Yes, Indian nationals require permits for North Sikkim and Nathula Pass. We assist with all permit arrangements as part of our tour packages for a hassle-free experience.
